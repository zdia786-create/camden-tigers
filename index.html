About Camden Tigers Sports Club

Inspiring youth
through sport

Founded in 2010 by Syed Karim Sayem and Rahel Bokht, alongside five dedicated co-founding members, Camden Tigers Sports Club was established with a clear mission: to inspire, engage and empower youth and teenagers through the power of sports.

Over the years, our club has become a vibrant hub for athletic excellence and community bonding. We actively organise and train young talent across multiple sports disciplines, including Football, Cricket and Badminton.

Since our inception, Camden Tigers Sports Club has successfully taken part in numerous local and regional tournaments, earning a reputation for discipline, teamwork and sportsmanship. We remain deeply committed to building a healthier, more active and united generation by instilling lifelong values through sports.
